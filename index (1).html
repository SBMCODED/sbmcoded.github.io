Quira: AI study app

An iOS and Android app that turns students’ own notes and textbooks into grounded summaries, quizzes, study plans and visual whiteboard lessons. I worked on the Django backend, document ingestion and retrieval, authentication, subscriptions, CI/CD and production operations, and built a replay harness that reruns real conversations 20 to 100 times per case to compare pass rates. The product was acquired by Rage Technology.
